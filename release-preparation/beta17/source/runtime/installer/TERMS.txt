Please read these terms. Ticking "I accept" and installing means you agree to them.

1. What this tool is
Smart Connector for Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD (Smart Connector) is an internal company tool. It connects Revit and AutoCAD on this computer to AI assistants (Codex and Claude) so they can read and, when you ask them to, change your open model or drawing. It also installs shared company workflow guidance ("skills") and keeps it up to date.

2. You stay in control of your work
The AI assistants only act when you ask them to in Codex or Claude. You are responsible for reviewing what they do before you save, issue or rely on any model, drawing or document. Keep normal backups and save your work before large automated changes.

3. Information reported to the administrator
When this computer joins the team service, the following is sent to your company administrator:
 - your name and email address, as you enter them during setup
 - the computer name and your Windows user name
 - the internet (IP) address the service sees, and when this computer last connected
 - the connector version
 - which connector skills and tools are used, as names and daily counts only
 - the skills you save that are submitted for review
The tool does NOT send your drawings, models, file names or paths, the text of your chats, or anything you type into Revit, AutoCAD, Codex or Claude. The administrator can view this information on a private dashboard and can block a computer. The dashboard is kept in the company's Notion workspace, where it is updated every hour; only the people who administer Smart Connector have access to it. Usage records are deleted from the team service after 180 days, and the dashboard shows the last 30 days.

4. Skills you share
Skills you save may be collected weekly (or when you choose Skills Sync) and sent to the administrator for review. Do not put client names, project data, passwords or personal information into skills. You can keep a personal skill private by listing it in the exclusion list described in the user guide. Nothing you submit is shared with the team until the administrator approves it.

5. Updates
The connector checks for approved updates once a day when Revit, AutoCAD or Smart Connector opens. When one is available you are asked whether to install it now or later; it installs when Revit and AutoCAD are closed. You can switch the check off in Settings.

6. Keep the team code private
Joining the team needs a team code from your administrator. Do not share the code, or a setup file that contains one, outside the company, upload it to public websites, or give it to anyone who is not a member of the team.

7. Acceptable use
Use the tool only for company work and in line with company policies, client agreements and the Autodesk and AI-provider terms that apply to you. Do not use it to access models, drawings or data you are not authorised to use.

8. No warranty
The tool is provided "as is" for internal use. The company does not guarantee that it, or the AI assistants it connects to, will be error-free. To the extent permitted by law, the company is not liable for losses arising from its use beyond what company policy provides.

These terms may be updated; you will be asked to accept a new version when it changes. Questions about the tool or the information it reports: contact your administrator.
